Panel · How This Is Scored
Each project starts at zero and picks up points only for factors that are actually true of it. Completed projects are always Green.

Staffing — no developer on file: +3
Priority — Critical: +2 · High: +1
Stage vs. priority — Critical or High priority still at Not Started or Requirements: +1
Fixed-deadline proximity — only applies when Deadline Fixed = Yes: past due: +3 · due within 30 days: +2 · due within 60 days: +1 · deadline is fixed but no date is on file: +1

Rating — 5 points or more is At Risk, 2–4 is Needs Attention, under 2 is On Track.
